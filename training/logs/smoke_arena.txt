Setup
-----
* Maps: the 88 tier 1 maps of data/maps/train that are one room with no door and no exit
  (2 to 12 monsters of every kind in the generator's legend, 2100 to 3500 tics):
    python -m training.rl.select_maps --maps data/maps/train/manifest.jsonl \
        --out data/maps/smoke_arena.jsonl --tier 1 --exits 0 --max-doors 0 --max-rooms 1
  With no exit an episode ends by death or at the tick cap, and the only rewards are the combat terms.
* Network: the real PolicyNet (1.31 M parameters, K = 12), real odometry, memory map and encoder
  in the workers, action masks from spec.action_masks in rollout and loss.
* 3 worker processes x 16 environments, rollout 64 steps (3,072 steps per iteration), 204
  iterations in 10 minutes. Learner on MPS (Apple GPU), workers on the CPU. The machine was
  shared and heavily loaded (load average 55 to 95 on 15 cores): 1,074 env steps/s overall
  (collection 1,735 steps/s, learner 8,515 samples/s, 38 % of the time in the update). The 19:24
  run used 6 workers and measured 1,088 overall: twice the workers did not make the loop faster.
* PPO: lr 7e-4, 3 epochs, 48 chunks of 16 steps per minibatch (12 gradient steps per iteration),
  gamma 0.99, lambda 0.95, clip 0.2, KL stop at 0.045. Curiosity off (int_coef 0, intrinsic disabled).
* Reward: the scorer-shaped extrinsic terms (kill, damage, death, ammo) plus hit_bonus 0.2 per
  own shot that hits a monster.
* Start of training: random initialisation, plus an offset of (+2.5, -2.5) on the output bias of
  the weapon head (head_bias in the config), so "next weapon" starts at 0.7 % of steps instead of 50 %.

    python -m training.rl.train --maps data/maps/smoke_arena.jsonl --config training/rl/configs/smoke_arena.json \
        --workers 3 --out runs/rl/smoke_arena_v2 --max-minutes 10
    python -m training.rl.play --maps data/maps/smoke_arena.jsonl --checkpoint runs/rl/smoke_arena_v2/latest.pt \
        --workers 3 --envs-per-worker 16 --episodes-per-row 4 --seed 1
    python -m training.rl.play --maps data/maps/smoke_arena.jsonl --random --workers 3 --envs-per-worker 16 \
        --episodes-per-row 4 --seed 1

What it took (each run 10 minutes, same maps, same machine)
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Defaults (lr 3e-4, 256 chunks per minibatch = 6 gradient steps per iteration): KL per update
   0.0004, entropies unchanged after 60,000 steps. Too few gradient steps. Stopped.
2. 32 gradient steps per iteration, lr 7e-4, default reward, no offset: 1.49 kills/episode after
   595,968 steps (1.3 times the baseline), still rising slowly; episodes 139 -> 197 steps.
3. As 2 with hit_bonus 0.1: the same curve (1.2 to 1.4 at 307,200 steps), stopped at 5 minutes.
   Own hits per episode were 0.4 at the start and 1.0 at 300,000 steps.
4. Diagnosis: the untrained policy presses "next weapon" on half of all steps, so the pistol is
   almost never ready. Measured with fixed random policies on these maps (144 episodes each):
     uniform random                    0.33 own hits/episode   1.12 kills
     "next weapon" on 5 % of steps     1.51 own hits/episode   1.47 kills
     "next weapon" never               3.94 own hits/episode   1.60 kills
   So nearly every kill of the random policy comes from monsters fighting each other, and the
   kill reward at the start is almost unrelated to the agent's own actions.
5. This run: weapon-head offset and hit_bonus 0.2. Own hits per episode 1.8 -> 19.7, kills
   1.37 -> 3.09, deaths 98 % -> 39 %.

Ablation, 19:51-20:01: the same run with hit_bonus 0, i.e. only the scorer-shaped terms
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Also above twice the baseline: 2.76 +- 0.11 kills per episode (2.4 times), 74 % of monsters,
43 % deaths after 724,992 steps. It learns more slowly at first (1.51 kills at 129,024 steps
against 2.02 with the hit term) and its curve is flat over the last third (share of monsters
killed 0.70 -> 0.70, against 0.73 -> 0.82 with the hit term). So the weapon-head offset is what
makes the arenas learnable; the hit term adds speed and a higher level.
