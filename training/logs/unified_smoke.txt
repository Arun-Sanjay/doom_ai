One-loop training check: the student drives, the teacher labels, loss = PPO + distillation
====================================================================================

Log: training/logs/unified_smoke.csv (one row per iteration). Config: training/configs/unified_smoke.json
(explained in unified_smoke.txt next to it). 26 Nov, on a 15-core laptop shared with other jobs.

Setup
  From random initialisation (PolicyNet defaults, strict goal bottleneck, 1.31 M parameters).
  6 worker processes x 16 environments, learner on the Apple GPU, 6144 steps per iteration,
  4 epochs x 6 minibatches of 1024 samples.
  Training maps: the 258 maps of tiers 0 and 2 in data/maps/train/manifest.jsonl that have no
  monsters and a start on the 64-unit grid (182 of tier 0, 76 of tier 2), half of the episodes each.
  Teacher mixing: beta 1.0 -> 0.2 over the first 700,000 steps, then 0.2. Distillation weight 1.
  Tick cap terminal (ppo.bootstrap_on_truncation false).
  The run stopped at its 1.5 M step budget after 19.7 minutes (245 iterations, 1,505,280 steps).
  Training and every evaluation below ran from one frozen copy of the code (other areas of the
  repository were being edited during the evening, see "Earlier runs").

Why agreement stops near 0.8 on the student's own trajectories (what was looked at)
  * Label alignment and masks are right: driving with the stored labels reaches the exit (test),
    replaying stored chunks reproduces the stored log-probabilities exactly with teacher-forced
    manager choices (test), and no USE label fell on a step where the mask forbids it.
  * By the teacher's steering mode, on teacher-driven steps (run 3 checkpoint, 10 minutes in):
        slow (44 % of steps)   move 0.79  strafe 0.81  turn 0.74
        look (26 %)            move 0.82  strafe 0.94  turn 0.96
        run  (14 %)            move 0.83  strafe 0.93  turn 0.75
        door (14 %)            move 0.91  strafe 0.94  turn 0.96
    The teacher steers with a look-ahead on stopping distance and with heading thresholds of one turn
    step. Near those thresholds two keys are equally good, and the student cannot place the threshold
    exactly; most disagreement is there.
  * The value field was the bottleneck of the first attempt. With field_coef 1 the squared error of
    the field (0.04) carried almost no gradient next to the cross-entropies: the learned field named
    the same best neighbouring cell as the teacher's on 24 % of manager steps (8 neighbours, chance
    12 %). With field_coef 10: 62 %, error 0.014, and the target pointer learned faster (exact target
    0.47 against 0.39 after about six minutes).
  * The manager does condition the controller, but most steps are decided by the sensors: replaying
    teacher-driven steps with the target mirrored through the crop centre lowers agreement from
    0.868 to 0.846, with another sub-goal to 0.858.

Throughput with labels on
  Workers alone (random actions, no network, 6 workers x 16 environments): 4,069 and 4,170 steps/s
  without labels, 3,636 and 3,692 with: 11 % less. Per environment step the label and its storage
  cost 0.34 ms of 2.5 ms (machine at load average 67).
  Whole loop in the reported run: 1,280 environment steps/s on average (2,844 while collecting; the
  PPO update takes 55 % of the time; learner 9,300 samples/s). No environment reset, no odometry
  error and no teacher error in 1.5 M steps.
