Full-agent decision time: DoomAgent.act() on one thread
========================================================

Measured with training/bench/agent_latency.py: the agent plays three maps of the frozen suites
(6x6, 19x15 and 27x28 cells) with its own actions, 700 decisions per map, 3 repeats, with
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 set before torch loads. Only the time
inside act() counts. Limits: the scorer allows 40 ms per decision averaged over a map; this
project holds itself to 20 ms mean on an x86 CI core and aims at 8 ms on the development Mac.

   load average at the start: 6.5 (1 min), 17.5 (5 min), 29.6 (15 min) on 15 cores: other jobs
   were running, so these are upper bounds for an idle machine.
   weights: policy network with initial weights (no policy.pt was shipped yet), exit detector
   sha256 8985406ed0a6. The cost of a pass does not depend on the weights.
