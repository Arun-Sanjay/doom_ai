unified_smoke.json: the 25-minute check that the one-loop training teaches navigation
====================================================================================

Run on a laptop (6 worker processes x 16 environments, learner on the Apple GPU) from random
initialisation. Results: training/logs/unified_smoke.txt and unified_smoke.csv.

    python -m training.rl.select_maps --maps data/maps/train/manifest.jsonl --out data/maps/unified_smoke_train.jsonl \
        --tiers 0,2 --max-monsters 0 --on-grid                 # 258 monster-free maps the teacher labels
    python -u -m training.rl.train --maps data/maps/unified_smoke_train.jsonl \
        --config training/configs/unified_smoke.json --out runs/rl/unified_smoke --max-minutes 25

  rollout 64 x 96 environments = 6144 steps per iteration; minibatch_chunks 64 (1024 samples) and 4
  epochs give 24 gradient steps per iteration.
  teach: beta 1.0 -> 0.2 over the first 700,000 steps (about half of what 25 minutes collect here).
  distill.levels [1.0]: the weight stays 1; the held-out measurement is made after the run.
  curriculum: one stage, tier 0 and tier 2 half each, no thresholds.
  ppo.bootstrap_on_truncation false: the tick cap is terminal.
  total_steps 1.5 M spans the learning-rate schedule (5e-4 -> 1.5e-4).

The proof gate of this check, and how it is recorded
  The check had two marks: (a) the distillation loss falls and the policy agrees with the teacher on
  at least 85 % of labelled choices; (b) the policy alone exits at least 50 % of held-out maps. What
  the run of training/logs/unified_smoke.txt (1.5 M steps, 19.7 minutes) reached, with the
  definition under which each mark is and is not met:

  (a) MET ONLY for the mean over labelled head choices on states the teacher drives; not met
      for whole steps, not met on the policy's own states, never met by the training log's column.
        python -m training.rl.agree --checkpoint CKPT --maps HELDOUT --beta 1 --steps 192
                                         per labelled head   move    strafe   turn    USE     all four heads at once
        teacher drives (--beta 1)             0.877          0.850   0.845    0.816   0.996        0.626
        policy drives  (--beta 0)             0.726          0.624   0.666    0.650   0.966        0.342
        column "agree" of the training log    0.827 at its best (beta 0.76); 0.73 to 0.80 once beta is 0.2 (0.78 over the last 20 iterations)
      "Per labelled head" pools the four labelled heads; USE is "off" on nearly every step, so the
      pooled number is above each of move, strafe and turn. The distillation loss fell from 8.91 to
      3.1-3.5.
  (b) MET on the tiers the run trained on (0 and 2): 155 exits in 240 episodes, 0.65.
        python -m training.rl.eval_alone --checkpoint CKPT --maps HELDOUT --episodes 2 --seed 11   (and --seed 12)
        tier 0     94 / 120   0.78
        tier 2     61 / 120   0.51      (0.57 and 0.45 in the two runs of 60 episodes: at the mark, not clear of it)
      NOT met on tiers 3 and 4 of dev_nav_v1, which this run never trained on: 8 / 40 and 17 / 40
      (0.20 and 0.43); with them the three-tier set gives 50 / 120, 0.42. That figure measures tiers
      outside the run and is not the gate.
      Through the real agent (submission/agent.py with the exported weights and the exit detector
      network, one episode per map): 42 / 60, 0.70 (tier 0 25 / 30, tier 2 17 / 30).

  HELDOUT for the numbers above: 60 monster-free maps, 30 each of tiers 0 and 2, generated from seeds
  700000 to 700050 (the training manifest uses seeds 0 to 300), all with the start on the 64-unit
  grid, so every step is labelled. Both agreement rows and both eval_alone runs were measured by the
  reviewer of the loop and repeated afterwards with the same commands: the same numbers to the last
  digit and the same counts per tier (the measurements are deterministic for a seed).

  For the long run the same two measurements are the yardstick, per tier: LAUNCH.txt, step 5. The
  "agree" column of the training log is not: it is taken on trajectories that the policy drives
  more and more, and it falls while imitation improves.
