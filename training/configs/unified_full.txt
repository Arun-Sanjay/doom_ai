unified_full.json: one-loop training on every tier, starting from a combat checkpoint
==================================================================================

Same loop, teacher, mixing, loss and held-out gate as unified_nav.json (see unified_nav.txt for
each setting). What differs:

    --maps    data/maps/train/manifest.jsonl          (all five tiers)
    --heldout data/maps/dev/manifest.jsonl            (training/suites/dev_v1.jsonl rebuilt, monsters included)

Curriculum: arenas stay in the mix at every stage, so combat is not forgotten
  Stage budgets in environment steps (threshold 2.0 can never be met: while beta is high the exit
  rate in training is the teacher's and says nothing about the student).
                tier 0   tier 1   tier 2   tier 3   tier 4
    nav          70 %     30 %                                  first 15 M steps
    doors        25 %     25 %     50 %                         next 25 M
    keys         10 %     20 %     30 %     40 %                next 40 M
    gauntlet      5 %     20 %     15 %     20 %     40 %       next 60 M
    adaptive      tiers 1-4, weight (1 - recent exit rate) + 0.3 each, from 140 M steps on
  adaptive_floor 0.3 (default 0.1) keeps every tier, the arenas included, above roughly 12 % of the
  episodes however well it goes. About half of the arenas have no exit: there the only way to
  score is to fight.

On steps with a visible enemy the teacher gives no controller labels and the executed action is
always the student's, so in arenas and in fights on the other tiers the controller is trained by
PPO alone. The manager still gets its label there (sub-goal "fight", target = the enemy's cell).

Distillation weight
  min_rate 0.3 (unified_nav: 0.5): the held-out maps contain monsters, where the teacher alone
  exits about one map in three, so 0.5 would hold the weight at 1 for too long. tolerance 0.06 with
  384 episodes, as in unified_nav. 14 of the 80 dev maps have no exit; the rate is over the others.
