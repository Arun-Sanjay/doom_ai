unified_nav.json: one-loop training of navigation (tiers 0, 2 and 3 of the training manifest)
==========================================================================================

What the run does. The student network drives every environment. In the rollout workers the
navigation teacher (training/teacher) labels each observation it can; the loss is PPO plus a
distillation term to those labels. Start it from a combat checkpoint with --init.

    --maps    data/maps/train/manifest.jsonl          (only tiers 0, 2, 3 are sampled, see curriculum)
    --heldout data/maps/dev_nav/manifest.jsonl        (training/suites/dev_nav_v1.jsonl rebuilt: monster-free dev maps)

Sizes
  workers 96 x envs_per_worker 12 = 1152 environments, rollout 64: 73,728 steps per iteration.
  ppo.minibatch_chunks 256 (4096 samples), 3 epochs: 54 gradient steps per iteration.
  total_steps 400 M spans the learning-rate (3e-4 -> 6e-5) and entropy schedules; stop earlier with
  SIGTERM (a final checkpoint is written) or --max-minutes.

Teacher (vec.teacher = "real")
  Labels: move, strafe, turn and USE on every step without a visible enemy; sub-goal and target on
  manager steps (every 8 steps); the travel-step field to the target on those steps. Attack and
  weapon are never labelled. No labels at all on maps whose start is off the 64-unit grid (about
  39 % of the training manifest: start_offset, or start_angle not a multiple of 90); those maps are
  trained by reinforcement alone and show the gap in the log (exit_rate_labelled / exit_rate_unlabelled).

Mixing (teach)
  beta = probability that the teacher's action is executed on a labelled step, and on a manager
  step that the manager's sub-goal and target are forced to the teacher's. 1.0 at the start, linear
  to 0.1 over the first 30 M steps, then 0.1. Forced steps are left out of the PPO policy term and
  kept in the value and distillation terms. Mixing stops when the distillation weight reaches 0.

Loss (ppo)
  distill_coef: weights of the six heads (attack and weapon have no labels, so their entries do
  nothing), the sub-goal and the target cell (0.5; it is a 4096-way choice with a large loss).
  field_coef 10, field_gamma 0.95: the network's value field is regressed on 0.95 ** (teacher's
  travel steps) on manager steps where the target in force is the teacher's. The squared error is
  small next to the cross-entropies (0.02 to 0.04 against 2 to 8), so at coefficient 1 it hardly
  moved the value propagation: in the laptop check the field then pointed to the right neighbouring
  cell on 24 % of manager steps, at coefficient 10 on 62 % (training/logs/unified_smoke.txt). With
  the strict goal bottleneck this field is the controller's only view of the route.
  bootstrap_on_truncation false: the tick cap is terminal, so running out of time costs the exit
  reward and leaving on time is learned from reward (the cap itself is not observable; the policy
  sees an assumed budget).

Starting from a combat checkpoint
  A combat checkpoint is very sure of its actions and its value field and target pointer have never
  been supervised: at the first update the heads' cross-entropy is about 40 and the field error about
  18 (2.9 and 0.7 from a random start), and the joint log-probability of the steps the policy chose
  itself moves by KL 6 in that one update (column kl_unlabelled; most of it is the manager's 4096-way
  target pointer being rewritten). Measured on the laptop from the combat checkpoint, 40 % tier 0 and
  60 % arenas, this did not cost the fighting: after 4 minutes the policy alone killed 0.68 of the
  monsters on the held-out arenas (0.31 for the checkpoint as it was, see training/logs/unified_smoke.txt).
  Two settings exist to slow that first phase down and are left off here because the same test
  showed no benefit from them (0.65 after 6 minutes, with slower distillation):
    ppo.lr_warmup N              the learning rate rises linearly over the first N updates
    ppo.target_kl_unlabelled X   an update ends once the policy has moved by more than 1.5 X (KL) on the
                                 steps it chose itself that carry no controller label
  Use them (20 and 0.02 were the values tried) only if kill_frac collapses in the first hour.

Distillation weight (distill), driven by the held-out exit rate
  Every heldout_every = 150 iterations (about 11 M steps) the policy alone plays one episode in each
  of heldout_envs = 384 environments on the --heldout maps with the agent's decoding and the
  evaluation budget. The weight steps down one level (1 -> 0.5 -> 0.25 -> 0.1 -> 0.03 -> 0) when that
  rate is at least min_rate and has not fallen more than tolerance below the rate at the last step
  down; two bad reports in a row step it back up.
  tolerance 0.06 (default 0.03): 384 episodes give a standard error of at most 0.026 per
  measurement, 0.036 for the difference of two, so 0.03 would be inside the noise.
  min_rate 0.5: while the policy alone exits less than half of the held-out maps the weight stays at 1.

Curriculum
  Stage budgets in environment steps, not success thresholds (threshold 2.0 can never be met):
  while beta is high the exit rate in training is the teacher's, so it says nothing about the student.
    nav_free   tier 0 only, the monster-free maps              first 15 M steps
    nav_doors  tier 0 40 %, tier 2 60 %                         next 25 M steps
    nav_keys   tier 0 20 %, tier 2 35 %, tier 3 45 %            until the end
  Tiers 2 and 3 contain monsters; fighting there comes from the combat checkpoint and from reward
  (vec.reward.hit_bonus 0.05 as in the combat run). Arenas are not sampled here: use unified_full
  when combat must be kept.
